# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Dual audience: (1) Students — school-age through working professionals — browsing, comparing, and applying to educational programs. They're evaluating quality, cost, and career outcomes. (2) Institutional decision-makers — university administrators, coaching institute owners, enterprise L&D teams — evaluating the platform for adoption. They need reliability, scalability, and proof of impact.

## Product Purpose

Elearn is a learning management system and program marketplace for Indian educational institutions. It connects students with school, diploma, undergraduate, postgraduate, and professional programs while giving institutions a platform to reach learners. Success means students find the right program and institutions fill their seats.

## Brand Personality

Playful, warm, trustworthy. The voice is approachable without being childish — think a knowledgeable friend who happens to love education. Tone leans casual-lowercase, uses concrete language over jargon, and keeps energy high without shouting.

## Anti-references

Corporate LMS platforms — sterile dashboards, stock photography, soulless blue-and-gray palettes, "synergy" copy. The opposite of a Blackboard or Canvas marketing page. Also avoids: gamified learning apps that feel childish, cluttered EdTech sites that overwhelm with noise, and anything that reads as a feature dump rather than a story.

## Design Principles

1. **Playful doesn't mean childish** — The schoolyard notebook aesthetic (cream paper, marker orange, sticker decorations) is warm and approachable but maintains academic credibility. Every playful element must earn its place.
2. **Show, don't tell** — Use visuals and interaction to demonstrate the learning experience rather than listing features. The notebook visual, sticker labels, and handwritten accents communicate more than bullet points.
3. **Trust through transparency** — Clear pricing, straightforward program details, no hidden fees. The design should feel honest and direct, building confidence in both students and institutions.
4. **Every learner matters** — From a Class 10 student to a working professional pursuing an MBA, the design accommodates all journeys without feeling generic or watered down.
5. **Warmth carries the brand** — Color warmth comes from the orange accent and cream canvas, not from default warm-tinted neutrals. Typography and imagery carry the emotional weight; the palette supports it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA compliance. Keyboard navigation throughout. Screen reader support for all interactive elements. Reduced motion alternatives for animations (prefers-reduced-motion). Color contrast ≥4.5:1 for body text, ≥3:1 for large text. Indian language support considered in type choices (Latin script primary, but font stack supports Devanagari fallbacks).
